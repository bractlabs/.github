# Community Code of Conduct

This policy applies to bractlabs public repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions.

## Expected Conduct

- Be respectful and considerate of people with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Give actionable feedback and accept corrections.
- Keep discussions relevant and make space for others to participate.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property and the distinction between the planned open-source game release and privately shared engine source.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, publication of private information, spam and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Do not post secrets, credentials or proprietary source without authorization.

## Reporting and Moderation

Report concerns privately to info@bractlabs.com. Include links and relevant context without unnecessarily repeating sensitive material.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, restrict participation or remove access when necessary. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reporters' personal information will not be shared publicly as part of moderation.

For questions about a moderation decision, contact info@bractlabs.com privately rather than restarting the dispute in public threads.

# Reporting a Security Concern

Please report security concerns privately to **info@bractlabs.com**, with the subject `Security report: Bract`.

Do not open a public issue or discussion for an unremediated security concern. Do not include live credentials, access tokens, personal information or proprietary engine source in the initial report.

Include the affected public URL or component, the date observed, your browser and operating system, reproduction steps and the potential impact. Use a minimal proof of concept without accessing other users' data or disrupting services.

Bract and Racer are pre-release software. This policy does not promise a response deadline, a bounty, a production support term or permission to test systems you do not own. Coordinate disclosure privately before publishing details.

Corporate engine-source recipients should also follow the reporting obligations in their agreements.
